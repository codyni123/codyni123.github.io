About the project

Red 10 is a six-player card game I took from first sketch to the App Store — game design, engineering, and every word of marketing, by one person.

It's a climbing game in the Big Two family with the twist that carries the whole product: teams are secret. Whoever draws the red 10s is quietly on a side, and alliances only reveal themselves through play. Rounds are fast, the table talk is loud, and no two deals feel the same.

Shipping it meant playing PMM for my own product — naming the app, writing the subtitle, art-directing the App Store screenshots, and tuning the listing — then iterating across fifteen releases since launch.

01

A hook you can say in five words

"Big Two with hidden teams" is the positioning, the App Store subtitle, and the pitch — one line that makes card players lean in and everyone else ask how it works.

02

Solo to social

A training mode against AI teaches the game hands-on; private rooms, ranked play, and leaderboards keep tables coming back. The funnel is built into the product.

03

Launched end to end

Store listing, screenshots, rules content, release notes — every touchpoint written and shipped solo, fifteen versions and counting.
