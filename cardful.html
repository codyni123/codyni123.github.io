About the project

Cardful is a credit-card rewards copilot — it does the math your wallet has been skipping.

Search a store and it ranks the cards you actually carry by what each one really earns: the multiplier times an honest cents-per-point value, reasoning shown. It tracks statement credits before they expire, puts every subscription on a calendar, and answers whether an annual fee earned its keep. No bank login required.

As a marketing exercise it went the full distance: category research, a freemium pricing model, a listing written to convert — and an App Review rejection diagnosed and turned into an approved release.

01

Positioned against the dread

The listing opens on the pain — "Your cards are full of money you are not using" — and every section pays it off with a concrete dollar saved.

02

Priced as a product

A free tier for the hook and Cardful Pro on subscription — a freemium model built around the first "it just saved me money" moment.

03

Shipped through App Review

Rejected on first submission under guideline 3.1.2; diagnosed, fixed, and approved — the unglamorous half of a real App Store launch.
