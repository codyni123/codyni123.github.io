About the project

Liar's Dice is the classic bluffing game rebuilt as a premium native iOS app — no ads, no tracking, just the game.

Five AI opponents who genuinely play differently across three difficulty tiers, pass-and-play on a single phone, and private four-letter rooms that work across networks. A live bid composer shows exactly what's legal as you build a bid — the interface teaches the game while you play it.

The launch leaned on a three-word campaign — Bid. Bluff. Call. — carried from the App Store screenshots through the product page. In a category full of ad-stuffed ports, positioning it premium was the entire go-to-market.

01

Three words, whole campaign

"Bid. Bluff. Call." anchors the screenshots, the subtitle, and the story — a launch narrative small enough to survive an App Store search result.

02

The interface is the tutorial

A bid composer that surfaces legal moves in real time replaced tutorial screens — new players learn the rules by playing their first round.

03

Premium as positioning

No ads, no tracking, native design through and through. Against a shelf of lookalike ports, restraint was the differentiator worth marketing.
